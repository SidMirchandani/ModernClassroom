# 01 Ask: the problem

## Who feels it
- Ms. Gomes (teacher).

## What she does today
- Google Classroom only.

## How often it hurts
- Daily.

## The pain, as she described it
- Re-posting the year: typing every assignment into Classroom by hand from her syllabus.
- Can't see who's where: no single view of each student's progress in a self-paced class.
- Plan keeps changing: when the syllabus shifts, updating Classroom item by item.
- Students race ahead or get lost: no way to pace or gate a self-paced class.

## Evidence
- Ms. Gomes asked for it herself.

## Problem statement
Ms. Gomes's year plan lives in her own files, but her students live in Google Classroom, and keeping the two in step is manual, repeated work. Because Classroom has no view of self-paced progress, she also can't tell daily who is ahead, behind or stuck.
